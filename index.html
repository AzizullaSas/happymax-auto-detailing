Built for island cars

Salt, sun and red dirt don’t stand a chance.

Hawaiʻi is beautiful — and brutal on paint. Every package treats the three things that age island cars fastest.

  • Salt air

    Ocean spray settles on every panel and starts corrosion in weeks. We neutralize it — wheel wells and undercarriage included.

  • UV index 11+

    Year-round sun bakes clear coat and cracks dashboards. Every detail finishes with UV-blocking protection inside and out.

  • Red dirt & sand

    Iron-rich dirt stains carpet and paint. pH-balanced iron removers and hot-water extraction lift it out instead of smearing it.

08 — FAQ

Good questions.

Something else? Text us at (808) 555-0147 — we usually reply in minutes.

Do I need to provide water or electricity?

No. Our rig carries 100 gallons of filtered, spot-free water and a quiet inverter generator. Just give us a parking spot with room to open the doors.

Can you detail in a condo or apartment garage?

Yes. Where running water isn’t allowed we use a rinseless wash method on drop mats — no runoff, no mess. We’ll double-check your building’s rules when we confirm.

How long does a detail take?

Express takes about 1.5 hours, Signature about 4 hours. Ceramic Pro is 1–2 days because the coating needs time to cure — the second visit is short.

Is ceramic coating worth it in Hawaiʻi?

Strong UV, salt air and vog are tough on clear coat. A 9H ceramic layer blocks UV fading, lets salt and red dirt rinse right off and keeps the gloss for years. If you plan to keep the car three years or more, it pays for itself.

What happens if it rains?

Light showers are fine — we work under a pop-up canopy. If it’s pouring, we reschedule for free and you pick the new slot.

How do I pay? Is there a deposit?

No deposit for Express or Signature. Ceramic Pro needs a $100 deposit, credited to your total. We take cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and cash after the job.
